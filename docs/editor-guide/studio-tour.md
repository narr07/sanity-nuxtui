# Studio tour

Open the Studio in your browser and log in. Locally it runs at `http://localhost:3333`; a hosted Studio has an address like `your-name.sanity.studio`.

## The menu

| Menu item | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Pages** | One-off pages built from blocks: Home, About, Pricing… |
| **Collections** | Repeating content such as Blog, Projects or Docs. Open one to see its entries and its settings |
| **All entries** | Every entry of every collection in one list, handy for searching |
| **Authors** | People shown on blog posts |
| **Site Settings** | Site name, logo, menu, footer, languages and interface text |

## The tools

The tabs at the top switch between three tools:

- **Structure**: edit content in forms. This is where you spend most of your time.
- **Presentation**: edit next to a live preview of the site. See [Preview and publish](./preview-and-publish.md).
- **Vision**: runs queries against the content. Meant for developers.

## Saving and publishing

You never need to save. Every change becomes a **draft** as you type, and the site does not change until you click **Publish** at the bottom right of the document.

- The **⋯** menu next to Publish can unpublish, duplicate or delete the document.
- The clock icon opens the **history**, where you can compare and restore earlier versions.
- A green dot next to a document means it is published; orange means it has unpublished changes.

> [!TIP]
> **Work on several pages at once.** Drafts are kept per document, so you can prepare a new page and its menu link, then publish both when you are ready.

## Finding things

Use the search box at the top of the Studio to find any document by title. In long lists, the sort menu (the arrows icon) orders documents by title or date.
