# Images

You can upload images wherever a field says *Image*, *Cover image* or *Photos*. The site resizes them for every screen and converts them to modern formats, so upload the largest version you have.

## Alternative text

Fill in **Alternative text** on every image. It describes the image for people who use a screen reader and for search engines. Write what the image shows in a short sentence: *"Two people reviewing a page layout on a laptop"*, not *"image1.jpg"*.

The Studio shows a warning on images without alternative text.

## Crop and hotspot

The same image appears in different shapes: wide on a desktop, square on a card, narrow on a phone. Tell the site which part matters:

1. Click the image, then **Edit** (the crop icon).
2. Drag the **hotspot** (the circle) over the important part, for example a face.
3. Optionally drag the **crop** handles to cut away edges you never want shown.

Every crop on the site keeps the hotspot in view.

## Captions

Images in rich text and in galleries can have a **Caption**, shown under the image. Use it for credits and context.

## Many photos at once

In a **Photos** field or an **Image Gallery** block, drag a whole selection of files onto the field. They upload together and keep their order; drag them to reorder.

## While images load

Visitors see a pixelated preview of each image until the full image arrives, so pages do not jump around while loading.
