# Preview and publish

## Presentation: edit on the page

Open **Presentation** at the top of the Studio. The site appears next to the editor, showing your drafts.

- **Click any text** on the page to open its field in the editor.
- **Drag blocks** on the page to reorder them.
- **Navigate** the site as usual; the editor follows the page you are on.
- Use the address bar of the preview to open any page, in any language.

Nobody else sees your drafts. Visitors keep seeing the published version until you publish.

## Publish

Click **Publish** on each document you changed. A page and the menu link pointing to it are two documents, so publish both.

After publishing, the live site updates within about a minute. Pages are cached for speed, and the cached copy refreshes in the background.

## Undo a change

- Before publishing: **⋯ → Discard changes** removes the draft.
- After publishing: open the **history** (clock icon), pick an earlier version and restore it, then publish again.
- To take a page offline without deleting it: **⋯ → Unpublish**.

> [!NOTE]
> **Preview not loading?** The preview needs a read token on the site. If you see a connection error, ask a developer to follow [Visual Editing](../developer-guide/visual-editing.md).
